    Each player has their own hero, stars, levels, spelling list, sidekick, and report. Switch players with the button at the top of the start screen.

    Staying on track

    Reading help

    Red/green glasses mode

    Glasses mode shows the important words in the color the training eye can see, so the eyes practice during reading. It is extra practice and does not replace a prescribed vision program. Check with your child's eye doctor before adding glasses time.

    When time is up, the game finishes the current mission, then asks for a 3-minute stretch break. A grown-up can skip it.

    Sync across devices

    Saved progress

    Add to your home screen

    iPhone or iPad: open the game in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen." Android, Chrome, or Edge: use the "Install as an app" button on the start screen or the browser menu. After it has been opened once, the game works without internet.
